About

Hello, I'm Gabriel Mukobi! I am an SI security researcher at the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) as well as a Ph.D. student in Computer Science on leave from UC Berkeley and advised by Jacob Steinhardt and Dawn Song. I completed my M.S. and B.S. in Computer Science at Stanford.

My other interests include games, music, 3D art, photography, film, virtual reality, watches, animals, and epic fantasy/sci-fi!
